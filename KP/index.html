India's Premier Wildlife Sanctuary

Home to the majestic Royal Bengal Tiger since 1936, Jim Corbett National Park is a testament to India's rich biodiversity.

A Legacy of Conservation

Established in 1936 as Hailey National Park, Jim Corbett is India's oldest national park and the birthplace of Project Tiger. Named after the legendary hunter-turned-conservationist Jim Corbett, the park spans 520.82 sq km in the foothills of the Himalayas in the Nainital district of Uttarakhand.

The park's diverse landscape — from dense sal forests and grasslands to river beds and marshy depressions — supports an incredible variety of flora and fauna. The Ramganga River flows through the park, creating a lifeline for its inhabitants.

Safari Zones

Explore Six Unique Zones

Each zone offers a distinct experience with varying terrain, wildlife density, and accessibility.

🏕️

Dhikala Zone

The crown jewel of Corbett. Vast grasslands, highest tiger sighting probability. Overnight stays available.

🌿

Bijrani Zone

Dense sal forests with diverse wildlife. Popular for jeep safaris. Excellent for bird watching.

☀️

Jhirna Zone

Open year-round. Grasslands and mixed forests. Great for winter visits when other zones close.

🌾

Dhela Zone

The newest eco-tourism zone. Buffer area with growing wildlife population. Budget-friendly option.

🏞️

Durga Devi Zone

Located at the northeast entrance. Hilly terrain along the Ramganga. Birder's paradise.

🌉

Garjiya Zone

Famous for the Garjiya Devi Temple on the riverbank. Accessible and scenic with diverse wildlife.

Wildlife

The Magnificent Inhabitants

From the elusive Royal Bengal Tiger to over 600 species of birds, Corbett is a biodiversity hotspot.

Royal Bengal Tiger

The undisputed king of Corbett. Over 230 tigers call this park home.

Asian Elephant

Herds of wild elephants roam the sal forests and river valleys.

Indian Leopard

The stealthy big cat, often spotted in the hilly terrains of Corbett.

Sambar Deer

The largest deer species in India, a common sight across all zones.

Avian Paradise

Over 600 bird species including kingfishers, eagles, and hornbills.

Visitor Information & Tips

Everything you need to know before visiting Jim Corbett National Park.

📅 Best Time to Visit

  • ✓ November – June — Park is open for tourism
  • ✓ February – May — Peak season, best for tiger sightings
  • ✓ November – February — Ideal for bird watching
  • ✓ July – October — Monsoon, most zones closed
  • ✓ Jhirna Zone — Open year-round

🎒 What to Carry

  • ✓ Government-issued photo ID (mandatory)
  • ✓ Binoculars and camera with zoom lens
  • ✓ Earth-toned clothing (khaki, green, brown)
  • ✓ Sunscreen, hat, and insect repellent
  • ✓ Light jacket (winters can be cold)
  • ✓ Water bottle and basic medicines

📋 Park Rules

  • ✓ No honking, loud music, or flash photography
  • ✓ Stay inside your vehicle at all times
  • ✓ No plastic, littering, or feeding animals
  • ✓ Follow the guide's instructions strictly
  • ✓ Maintain silence in the core zones

⏰ Park Timings

  • ✓ Morning Safari: 6:00 AM – 9:30 AM (Summer) / 7:00 AM – 10:00 AM (Winter)
  • ✓ Evening Safari: 2:00 PM – 5:30 PM (Summer) / 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM (Winter)
  • ✓ Night Safari: 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM
  • ✓ Entry Fee: ₹50 (Indian) / ₹500 (Foreign)
